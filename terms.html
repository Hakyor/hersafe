Terms of Service

Last updated: August 2026

Purpose of the platform

HerSafe exists to let people report harassment anonymously and to give communities aggregated, anonymous visibility into safety patterns. It is not a mechanism for naming, accusing, or shaming any named individual.

Prohibited content

You may not submit names, photos, social media handles, license plates, employee IDs, or any other detail that could identify a specific individual — whether the person you are describing or anyone else. Reports that include such details may be removed by administrators without notice.

Evidence links

You may optionally include links to your own Google Drive files as supporting context. You are solely responsible for what those files contain and who can access them. Do not link to files containing identifying imagery or information about any individual.

Acceptable use

Do not use HerSafe to harass, defame, threaten, or surveil another person. Do not submit false reports. Do not attempt to circumvent rate limits, scrape the platform, or interfere with its operation.

No legal advice

HerSafe is a community awareness tool, not a substitute for law enforcement, legal counsel, or emergency services. In an emergency, contact your local emergency number first.

Moderation

Administrators may remove any report that violates these terms, appears to be spam, or otherwise undermines the platform's anonymity guarantees.

Changes

These terms may be updated from time to time. Continued use of HerSafe after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised terms.

All community data is anonymous and aggregated.
