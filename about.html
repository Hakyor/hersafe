About HerSafe

Mission

To make everyday harassment visible as a pattern, without exposing any individual — so communities and institutions can respond to what the data shows.

Vision

A world where reporting harassment costs nothing and prevents more of it.

Privacy by design

No accounts are required to report. We never publish names, photos, or identifying details. Read our full Privacy Policy for details.

Open source

HerSafe's code is open for review, audit, and contribution.

Privacy Policy Terms

All community data is anonymous and aggregated.
