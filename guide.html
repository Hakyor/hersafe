Everyday safety tips

Share your live location with someone you trust when traveling alone.
Keep your phone charged and easily reachable, not buried in a bag.
Trust your instincts — leaving a situation early is never overreacting.
Note landmarks and well-lit routes in areas you visit often.

Frequently asked questions

Is my report really anonymous?

Yes. We never ask for your name, and reports are stored without any identifying information about you or the person involved.

Can I name the person who harassed me?

No. HerSafe is not a naming-and-shaming platform. Descriptions and evidence links must not include identifying details of any individual.

Who can see my evidence links?

Only you control access to your Google Drive files. HerSafe stores the link text only — never the file itself.
