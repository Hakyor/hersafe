Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

What we collect

When you submit a report, we store only: the incident type, an optional free-text description, an optional date/time, a location (city and/or coordinates), and any Google Drive links you choose to paste. We do not collect your name, email, phone number, or device identifiers, and reporting requires no account.

What we never collect

We never ask for or store the name, photo, social media handle, or any other identifying detail of any person you describe in a report. HerSafe is built so that identifying information about individuals — reporters or the people they describe — has no field to go into.

Evidence links

Google Drive links you paste are stored as plain text URLs only. We never fetch, copy, host, or view the files behind them. You remain solely responsible for the sharing permissions on your own Google Drive files.

How aggregated data is used

The Safety Map and Statistics pages show counts of reports grouped by area, time period, and incident type. These aggregates are designed so that no individual report can be singled out or re-identified.

Data retention and deletion

Administrators can remove spam or duplicate reports. Because reports carry no identifying information, we cannot process individual data-deletion requests tied to a person's identity — there is nothing identifying to delete.

Third parties

Map tiles are served by OpenStreetMap contributors. We do not sell or share report data with advertisers or data brokers.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the project maintainers listed in the GitHub repository.

All community data is anonymous and aggregated.
